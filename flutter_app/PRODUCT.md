# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Open source community: developers, hackers, technically literate people who track their own biology with the same detachment they'd apply to a codebase. They open Somnus after waking up or before sleeping, often alone, in a dark room. They don't want to be coached. They want the data, presented cleanly, so they can decide.

## Product Purpose

A local-first sleep tracker that computes what matters: debt, circadian regularity, DLMO, social jet lag. No cloud. No account. No telemetry. The software runs on your machine, stores data on your machine, and knows nothing about you it didn't learn from you. Privacy is not a feature, it is the architecture.

## Brand Personality

Dark. Magnetic. Minimal. Cold. Mechanical.

Seduces but doesn't embrace. Instrument-grade precision. The kind of software that respects you enough to show you the truth without softening it.

Voice: terse, direct, technical without being verbose. Never warm. Never motivational. Closest reference tone: factory.ai meets Urbit documentation.

## References

- **factory.ai**: exact aesthetic template, mechanical dark, strong type, cold accent. Substitute dark green for orange.
- **Urbit**: privacy-first ethos, eclectic and idiosyncratic minimalism, almost anti-consumer, cult of craft.
- **Linear**: instrument-grade product precision; everything in its place, nothing decorative.

## Anti-references

- Apple Health: soft gradients, rounded blobs, consumer warmth. Wrong register entirely.
- Sleep Cycle / Oura Ring app: wellness-brand aesthetics, feel-good copy, encouraging tone.
- Notion / typical SaaS dark mode: friendly neutrals, gradient cards, "modern" safety.
- Any interface with gradient text, glassmorphism cards, or hero-metric templates.

## Design Principles

1. **Instrument over app.** Somnus behaves like a precision tool, not a companion. UI serves data retrieval, not emotional reassurance.
2. **Privacy as identity.** Local-first is not a checkbox. Surface it in the UI so it's impossible to miss.
3. **Cold precision over warm comfort.** Show the number. Don't cushion it with color-coded encouragement or motivational copy.
4. **Eclectic restraint.** One unusual deliberate choice per surface, not zero and not ten. The interface should feel authored, not assembled.
5. **Earn every element.** If a component, label, or animation doesn't carry information or improve interaction, it doesn't ship.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Color contrast checked against the dark green accent. No accessibility-hostile choices (pure black text on white, pure white on black).
